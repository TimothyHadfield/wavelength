How to play
  1. Each turn, one player is the Hinter. Only they see where the target is on the dial.
  2. They give a clue that sits that far between the two words on the card.
  3. Their team turns the dial to where they think the target is.
  4. The other team calls whether the target is further left or right.
  5. Reveal: centre band 4, next bands 3 and 2. A right left/right call gives the other team 1 (not when the guess scores 4).
  6. First team to the target score wins. The team that goes second starts with 1 point.

Co-op (Settings): everyone is one team for 7 cards, no left/right call; a bullseye scores 3 and adds a card. 16+ wins.
